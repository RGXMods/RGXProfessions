# RGX | Professions v0.3.2

Requires **RGX-Framework 2.15.2 or later**.

- New guide page. The step you are on shows the crafts (or points) still to go, a one-line instruction, when the recipe stops giving skill-ups, and a progress bar through the step. Materials show what you have against what you need, with item tooltips; the Where card puts the nearest trainer or vendor on your map with one click.
- Profession tiles show your skill and a progress bar and keep a steady size as the window resizes.
- Materials to Max is a compact overlay with a tab for each rank, a totals bar and a Material / Have / Need list. Resize it from the corner, scale it with Ctrl and the mouse wheel, and lock it so Escape does not close it.
- A settings gear in the book opens a settings window with a minimap icon on/off switch.
- Recipe and reagent icons now use the spell and item APIs WoW Forever provides, and the guide's notes match the WoWSoD Pro guide.
- The look comes from the framework's RGX Material Round style in RGXProfessions' own colours.
