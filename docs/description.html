# RGX | Profession Leveling Guide ## RGX Mods - [RealmGX](https://realmgx.com) Community Project **"The profession leveling bible." - Now in World of Warcraft!** *** ## Overview **RGX | Profession Leveling Guide** is the profession leveling guide for WoW Forever. Open the book, pick a profession, and see exactly what to craft or gather next, how many of it, what it takes, and where to get it. In game the addon shows up as RGX | Professions, built on the [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) RGX Material Round interface style in this addon's own colours. It is a rebrand and extension of PLG by LirameiRav, with current WoW Forever routes, reagents, notes, and tips. **The Kiwi Says:** "Open the book, follow the page, watch the bars fill. Bwwiiiee." *** ## Features The Book - One window with a tile for each of the twelve professions, showing your live skill and a progress bar. Click a tile to open its 1-300 leveling path. Next Up, At A Glance - The step you are on shows the number of crafts (or points) still to go, the instruction in one line, when the recipe stops giving skill-ups, and a progress bar through the step. Materials With Live Counts - Every reagent for the step with what you have against what you need, an item tooltip on hover, and a total for everything left to reach the cap. Materials To Max - A compact overlay with a tab for each rank still to come, a totals bar, and a Material / Have / Need list. Drag the corner to resize it, hold Ctrl and scroll to scale it, and use the lock to keep it open when you press Escape. Where To Get It - The nearest trainer or vendor for your faction with its zone and coordinates. Click it to put a waypoint on your map. Step List And Tooltips - Every step is in the list with its skill range and colour; hover for the recipe tooltip and the materials it needs. Settings - The gear in the book's header opens a small window: minimap icon on/off, open the guide with a profession window, and select recipes when you click them. Minimap Button - Left-click opens the book, drag moves it, right-click opens the settings, Ctrl+right-click hides it. Live Trade-Window Guide - When a profession window is open, the step-by-step guide appears beside it. *** ## Slash Commands
/prof # Open or close the book
/prof book # Open the book
/prof show # Open the trade-window guide
/prof show # Open the guide for a profession
/prof preview # Preview the steps for a skill level
/prof tips # Print what Forever changed
/prof settings # Open the settings window
/prof icon on|off # Show or hide the minimap button
*** ## Compatibility WoW Forever|Fully Supported RGX | Professions targets WoW Forever (Interface 16001) and requires [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) v2.15.2 or newer. The addon draws no interface of its own: its windows, cards, headers, and buttons come from the framework's RGX Material Round style, in this addon's own colours, and no other addon's theme is touched. Twelve Locales|enUS, deDE, esES, esMX, frFR, itIT, koKR, ptBR, ptPT, ruRU, zhCN, zhTW Every user-visible string comes from the addon's own localization table, selected by the client's locale with automatic fallback to English. *** ## Installation **Step 1:** Install the required dependency [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) (v2.15.2+). **Step 2:** Copy the RGXProfessions folder into WoW Forever/Interface/AddOns. **Step 3:** Reload with /reload or restart the client, enable both addons, and type /prof. *** ## Data And Credits * PLG (Profession Leveling Guide) - by LirameiRav, updated from the original Retail version by Keg, is the addon this one grew from. * Classic-era vendor, trainer and quest data - from [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com). * WoW Forever routes, reagents, notes and tips - from [WoWSoD Pro](https://wowsod.pro/wow-forever/professions/leveling). * Rebranded and extended - by DonnieDice for [RealmGX](https://realmgx.com). *** ## Support the Project * **Buy Me a Coffee:** [Donate](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/donniedice) * **Donate via CashApp:** [Donate](https://bit.ly/3fyxxSU) * **Star on GitHub:** [RGX | Professions](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGXProfessions) *** ## Feedback and Contributions * **Report Issues:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGXProfessions/issues) * **Contribute:** Open an issue or a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGXProfessions). * **Contact:** Join the [RealmGX Discord](https://discord.gg/N7kdKAHVVF). *** ## License This project is licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGXProfessions). *** ## Disclaimer **Disclaimer:** This RGX | Profession Leveling Guide addon is provided "as is" without warranty.
