## Overview

**RGX | Profession Leveling Guide** is the profession leveling guide for **WoW Forever**. Open the book, pick a profession, and see exactly what to craft or gather next, how many of it, what it takes, and where to get it.

In game the addon shows up as *RGX | Professions*, built on the [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) RGX Material Round interface style in this addon's own colours. It is a rebrand and extension of PLG by LirameiRav, with current WoW Forever routes, reagents, notes, and tips.

**The Kiwi Says:** *"Open the book, follow the page, watch the bars fill. Bwwiiiee."*

## Features

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **The Book** | **Next Up, At A Glance** | **Materials With Live Counts** |
| One window with a tile for each of the twelve professions, showing your live skill and a progress bar. | The step you are on shows the crafts still to go, the instruction in one line, when the recipe stops giving skill-ups, and a progress bar. | Every reagent for the step with what you have against what you need, an item tooltip on hover, and a total for everything left to reach the cap. |
| **Materials To Max** | **Where To Get It** | **Step List And Tooltips** |
| A compact overlay with a tab for each rank still to come, a totals bar, and a Material / Have / Need list. Resizable and scalable, with a lock. | The nearest trainer or vendor for your faction with its zone and coordinates. Click it to put a waypoint on your map. | Every step is in the list with its skill range and colour; hover for the recipe tooltip and the materials it needs. |
| **Settings** | **Minimap Button** | **Live Trade-Window Guide** |
| The gear in the book's header opens a small window: minimap icon on/off, open the guide with a profession window, and select recipes when you click them. | Left-click opens the book, drag moves it, right-click opens the settings, Ctrl+right-click hides it. | When a profession window is open, the step-by-step guide appears beside it. |

## Slash Commands

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/prof` | Open or close the book |
| `/prof book` | Open the book |
| `/prof show` | Open the trade-window guide, or the profession menu if no window is open |
| `/prof show <profession>` | Open the guide for a profession (partial names work) |
| `/prof preview <profession> <skill>` | Preview the steps for a skill level |
| `/prof tips <profession>` | Print what Forever changed, tips and kit for a profession |
| `/prof settings` | Open the settings window |
| `/prof icon on/off` | Show or hide the minimap button |

## Compatibility

- **WoW Forever** | Fully Supported
- RGX | Professions targets WoW Forever (Interface `16001`) and requires [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) `v2.15.2` or newer. The addon draws no interface of its own: its windows, cards, headers, and buttons come from the framework's RGX Material Round style, in this addon's own colours, and no other addon's theme is touched.
- **Twelve Locales** | enUS, deDE, esES, esMX, frFR, itIT, koKR, ptBR, ptPT, ruRU, zhCN, zhTW

Every user-visible string comes from the addon's own localization table, selected by the client's locale with automatic fallback to English.

## Installation

1. **:** Install the required dependency [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) (`v2.15.2`+).
2. **:** Copy the `RGXProfessions` folder into WoW Forever `Interface/AddOns`.
3. **:** Reload with `/reload` or restart the client, enable both addons, and type `/prof`.
